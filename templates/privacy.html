Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

01Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. When you use Spendly, we store the expenses you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect bank account, card, or payment details.

02How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to run Spendly for you: signing you in, showing your expenses, and generating summaries and category breakdowns. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as described in this policy.

03Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and is accessible only through your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active; when you delete expenses or your account, the related data is removed.

04Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when pages load. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If we add other services in the future, we will update this policy.

05Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@example.com.
